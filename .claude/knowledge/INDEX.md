## Domain Summaries

### I Ching

Four files cover I Ching comprehensively. `commentary.md` provides historical context, interpretive traditions (Confucian, Daoist, Western, Vietnamese), and the philosophical framework (Ten Wings, synchronicity, Tao of Change). `trigrams.md` documents all 8 trigrams with complete correspondences. `methods.md` gives step-by-step casting procedures (yarrow stalk, coin, simplified, Plum Blossom). `hexagrams-01-32.md` and `hexagrams-33-64.md` cover all 64 hexagrams with full multi-source commentary. All hexagram files include Vietnamese commentary [vi] tagged with `<!-- src: viet -->`. Sources span Alfred Huang, Wen Benebell, Vietnamese scholar Thu Giang Nguyễn Duy Cần, Wilhelm, and the Wen Zhouyi tradition.

### Numerology

Three files cover the Pythagorean/Millman numerology system in Vietnamese and English. `life-path.md` handles life path calculation and meaning for all primary numbers. `birth-date.md` handles the 3×3 grid, individual digit analysis, arrows of personality, birth day number, and personal year cycles. `name-numerology.md` handles soul urge, personality, expression/destiny numbers, name chart construction, name changing principles, and Mandala archetypes. The system is bilingual: Vietnamese terms (Con số chủ đạo, Mũi Tên, etc.) are paired with English equivalents throughout.

### Tarot

Three files cover the Marseille tarot tradition (CBD Tarot de Marseille, Nicolas Conver 1760 deck restored by Yoav Ben-Dov). `major-arcana.md` documents all 22 Major Arcana cards with visual, symbolic, and interpretive detail. `minor-arcana.md` documents all 56 Minor Arcana cards across four suits. `spreads.md` covers the open reading method, three-card spread, row spread, session protocol, inversion handling, and reading techniques. All content is in English; Marseille French titles are included for each card.

### Astrology

One file covers astrology. `fundamentals.md` documents all 12 zodiac signs in detail, each with Vietnamese name, date range, axis (Mind/Soul/Body), element (Fire/Earth/Air/Water), traits, challenges, life lessons, monthly birth distinctions, and remedial guidance. The file is primarily in Vietnamese [vi] but zodiac signs are labeled with both Vietnamese and English names. The file establishes the connection between astrology and Pythagorean numerology through the shared three-axis framework.
