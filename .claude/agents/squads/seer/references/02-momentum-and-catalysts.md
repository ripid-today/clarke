# Momentum and Catalysts — Seer Reference

Finding the right company is only half the job. Seer must also identify the right time to enter — the moment when the wind is at the thesis's back. This document covers momentum frameworks and the Vietnam-specific catalyst taxonomy that Seer uses to establish entry timing.

---

## 1. Dual Momentum — Gary Antonacci's Framework

Gary Antonacci's "Dual Momentum Investing" (2014) provides a rigorous, evidence-based approach to timing risk-on and risk-off positions. The core insight: momentum is the most persistent anomaly in financial markets. Assets that have outperformed continue to outperform over medium-term horizons (1-12 months). Seer uses dual momentum to confirm that entry timing is aligned with market momentum.

### Absolute Momentum (Trend Following)

Absolute momentum compares an asset's own returns to the risk-free rate (cash). If the asset has outperformed cash over the lookback period, it is in a positive momentum regime — risk-on. If it has underperformed cash, it is in a negative momentum regime — risk-off.

**Vietnam application:**

- The "risk-free" benchmark for a Vietnamese retail investor is the SBV savings deposit rate (currently approximately 5–7% per year for 12-month VND deposits)
- If a Vietnam stock has returned more than the deposit rate over the past 12 months → positive absolute momentum → green light for Seer
- If the stock has returned less than the deposit rate → negative absolute momentum → Seer should note this and reduce conviction score

**Lookback period for Vietnam:** The standard 12-month lookback is appropriate for medium-term momentum. For shorter-term setups (catalyst-driven trades), use a 3-month lookback — Vietnam's market moves faster than developed markets and 3 months captures the recent trend without too much noise.

### Relative Momentum (Cross-Sectional)

Relative momentum compares the asset's returns to its peers over the lookback period. If the stock has outperformed its sector peers and the broad market, it has relative momentum — this is a signal that money is specifically flowing into this stock, not just the sector.

**Vietnam benchmarks for relative momentum:**
- Broad market: VNINDEX or VN30 (depending on whether the stock is in VN30 or not)
- Sector benchmark: use the sector peer group from vietstock.vn or FiinGroup

**The dual momentum green light signal:**
Both absolute momentum (stock beat cash) AND relative momentum (stock beat peers) over the past 3-12 months → Seer can proceed with high confidence that entry timing is favorable.

**The dual momentum red flag:**
Absolute momentum negative (stock underperformed cash) → Seer reduces conviction score by 2 points, regardless of how compelling the fundamental thesis is. You may be right too early — and being too early in a momentum-negative stock is expensive.

## 2. Catalyst Taxonomy

Catalysts are specific, identifiable events or trends that will cause the market to re-price a stock. A thesis without a catalyst is just a description of why a company is good — not a reason why the stock will go up in the next 12 months.

Seer categorizes catalysts by type and timing to assign catalyst quality scores (used in opportunity scoring, references/03).

### Tier 1 Catalysts — Highest Scoring (Catalyst score 3-4/4)

These are catalysts that are specific, near-term (within 3-6 months), and likely to move the stock by more than 10%.

**Earnings surprise:**
- Definition: actual EPS exceeds consensus estimate by >10%
- Timing: quarterly — happens every 3 months, predictable calendar
- Impact: typically +3-7% in the 1-3 sessions immediately following the announcement, with sustained recovery if guidance is also raised
- How to use it: identify companies where Seer believes the market consensus is too low. If micro-analyst shows recent earnings momentum and management has historically guided conservatively, an earnings surprise is a high-probability near-term catalyst.

**Management guidance raise:**
- Definition: company raises its revenue/EPS guidance for the full year
- Often accompanies Q2 or Q3 earnings releases when visibility improves
- Impact: more sustained than a single earnings beat — a guidance raise signals that management sees the next 1-4 quarters as better than expected
- How to identify in advance: look for companies where the macro environment is improving in their sector faster than their guidance assumed

**Index inclusion or re-weighting:**
- VNINDEX or VN30 inclusion triggers passive fund buying — a mechanically forced demand event
- MSCI Vietnam reclassification to Emerging Market (a long-anticipated event) would trigger massive passive inflows to all VN30 components
- FTSE Vietnam Index changes are similar
- Impact: 10-30% re-rating for included stocks depending on passive fund size

**FOL expansion:**
- When a company raises its foreign ownership limit (e.g., from 49% to 100% for non-strategic sectors)
- Triggers immediate institutional buying from funds previously blocked by the FOL cap
- Impact: very high, often 15-25% in the sessions following the announcement

---

### Tier 2 Catalysts — Good but Less Immediate (Catalyst score 2-3/4)

**New government contract or state procurement:**
- Vietnam's government is a major customer for construction, IT, healthcare, and energy companies
- Contract awards can re-rate revenue projections significantly
- Timing: less predictable — depends on government budget cycles
- Impact: 5-15% on the announcement; sustained if the contract size is material (>10% of annual revenue)

**Sector policy tailwind:**
- SBV rate cuts benefit banks (cheaper funding), real estate (cheaper mortgage rates), and leveraged companies (lower interest expense)
- Public investment acceleration benefits construction, materials, and engineering companies
- Resolution of a regulatory backlog (e.g., condo project approvals in HCMC) benefits real estate developers
- Timing: more structural than event-specific — momentum can build over 3-6 months

**New production capacity going online:**
- For manufacturing companies: a new factory coming online expands revenue capacity
- The catalyst is the first quarter where new capacity contributes revenue (not the announcement of the capacity)
- Most useful for industrial companies (steel, cement, chemicals, industrial parks)

---

### Tier 3 Catalysts — Background Tailwinds (Catalyst score 1-2/4)

**Macro improvement without a specific trigger:**
- "The macro is getting better" is a background tailwind, not a catalyst
- It helps but doesn't give a specific entry point or timing signal
- Score: 1/4 in the catalyst component

**Cheap valuation without an identified catalyst:**
- "This stock is cheap" is not a catalyst — it's a description
- A cheap stock can stay cheap for years without a catalyst to unlock value
- Score: 0/4 in catalyst component (valuation is scored separately in the opportunity scoring framework)

## 3. Vietnam-Specific Catalyst Signals

These are Vietnam market-specific leading indicators that signal when the overall environment is favorable or unfavorable for Seer's risk-on positioning.

### SBV Rate Decisions

The State Bank of Vietnam's refinancing rate and discount rate are the single most important macro catalyst signals for Vietnam equities.

**Rate cut → Risk-on signal for growth stocks:**
- When SBV cuts rates, borrowing costs fall for companies and consumers
- Banks' net interest margins initially compress but credit demand expands — net positive for bank earnings over 2-3 quarters
- Real estate benefits most directly: mortgage affordability improves
- The transmission to the real economy takes 3-6 months — enter stocks ahead of when the impact shows in earnings, not after
- Signal threshold: refinancing rate below 4.5% is historically associated with growth-positive equity environments

**Rate hold after a cut cycle → Neutral:**
- Rates staying low after a cut cycle is not a new catalyst — it's just continuation
- Score: 1/4 on catalyst unless the hold extends further accommodation beyond consensus expectations

**Rate hike → Risk-off, Seer scores down all positions:**
- Unexpected rate hikes compress equity multiples across the board
- Seer applies a blanket -2 points to all conviction scores when SBV is in an unexpected tightening cycle

---

### Public Investment Disbursement Data

Vietnam's government is a major infrastructure investor. The Ministry of Finance releases quarterly disbursement data — how much of the planned capital budget has actually been spent.

**Where to find it:** mof.gov.vn — quarterly government budget reports

**Signal interpretation:**
- Disbursement >85% of plan in a quarter → strong signal for construction, materials, and engineering demand
- Disbursement <60% of plan → bottleneck; delays in government projects reduce demand for these sectors

**Relevant companies:** construction contractors (Coteccons, Hoa Binh), building materials (BCC, HT1, VCS), engineering services.

---

### SBV Credit Growth Data

Vietnam's SBV sets an annual credit growth quota for the banking system. The monthly release of credit growth data signals whether the banking system is expanding or contracting.

**Where to find it:** sbv.gov.vn — monthly statistical reports

**Signal interpretation:**
- Credit growth >13%: expansion phase. Buy banks, real estate, consumer credit companies.
- Credit growth 8-13%: moderate growth. Neutral signal.
- Credit growth <8%: systemic tightening or underperformance vs. quota. Caution — real estate and leveraged companies face headwinds.

---

### Foreign Net Buying Streaks

Vietnam's foreign investor flows are closely tracked. Data is available daily from HOSE and HNX — net foreign buying or selling by value.

**Signal interpretation:**
- Foreign net buying for 10+ consecutive sessions: institutional validation. Seer can upgrade conviction score by +1.
- Foreign net selling for 10+ consecutive sessions: structural headwind. Even a bullish thesis faces valuation suppression if foreign investors are exiting. Seer should reduce conviction score by -1.

---

### GSO Trade Surplus Data

Vietnam's trade surplus (or deficit) is an indicator of manufacturing sector health.

**Where to find it:** gso.gov.vn — monthly customs/trade statistics

**Signal interpretation:**
- Trade surplus expanding quarter-over-quarter: manufacturing sector is producing more, FDI is converting to exports, industrial demand is rising. Positive for industrial parks, logistics, manufacturing companies.
- Trade surplus contracting significantly: domestic demand may be pulling in more imports (consumer-positive) but manufacturing may be under stress.

---

### PMI Manufacturing Index

Vietnam PMI is published monthly by S&P Global (formerly IHS Markit).

**Signal thresholds:**
- PMI > 52: Expansion territory. Favorable for manufacturing companies, industrial parks, logistics.
- PMI 50–52: Neutral. Modest growth continuing.
- PMI < 50: Contraction. Negative for manufacturing-linked companies.

**The 3-month trend matters more than any single reading.** A PMI that has moved from 48 → 49 → 51 is more bullish than a PMI that has moved from 53 → 52 → 51.

## 4. Risk-On Vietnam Market Signals — The Green Light for Seer

When all or most of these signals align, the Vietnam equity environment strongly favors Seer's risk-on posture:

1. VN30 is above its 200-day SMA (broad market in uptrend)
2. SBV refinancing rate below 4.5% or recently cut
3. Vietnam PMI manufacturing above 52 for 2+ consecutive months
4. Foreign net buying positive for 5+ sessions
5. VND stable or appreciating against USD
6. Credit growth above 13%

**When all 6 align:** Seer can present its strongest theses with maximum recommended position sizes.

**When 3-4 align:** Moderate risk-on. Seer recommends entering at 75% of maximum size.

**When 2 or fewer align or any macro-analyst has rated environment as Bearish:** Seer acknowledges the headwind explicitly in the thesis and scores catalyst quality down by -1.
