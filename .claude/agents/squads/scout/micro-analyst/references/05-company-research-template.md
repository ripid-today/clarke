# Company Research Template

## Purpose
This template defines the exact one-page output format for micro-analyst's company research summary. Every company analysis submitted to Scout dispatcher, Commander, Seer, and Planner must follow this format. Consistency enables Commander to rapidly synthesize inputs from macro-analyst and technical-analyst alongside this output.

---

## Template Structure

### Block 1: Header (Required — Complete All Fields)

```
COMPANY RESEARCH SUMMARY
========================
Company:   [Full legal name]
Ticker:    [TICKER]
Exchange:  [HOSE / HNX / UPCoM]
Sector:    [Primary sector: Banking / Real Estate / Manufacturing / Consumer / Technology / Energy / Other]
Sub-sector:[More specific: e.g., Private Bank, Residential RE Developer, Steel, Electronics Retail, etc.]

Market cap:     VND [X,XXX]B  |  USD equivalent: ~$[X]M  |  [Size: Large / Mid / Small cap]
Current price:  VND [X,XXX]
52-week range:  VND [X,XXX] (low) — VND [X,XXX] (high)
Date:           [YYYY-MM-DD]

KEY RATIOS:
P/E (trailing):     [X.X]×   |  Sector avg: [X.X]×
P/B:                [X.X]×   |  Own historical avg (5yr): [X.X]×
ROE (trailing):     [X.X]%   |  Threshold: >15% = quality
D/E:                [X.X]    |  Note: [conservative / moderate / elevated / high]

Margin of safety:          [X]%  (vs. current price)
Earnings quality flag:     [PASS / FLAG — reason]
```

---

### Block 2: Thesis — 3 Bullets (Required)

Each bullet must be specific, data-backed, and directly support why this company is an investment candidate. Vague claims ("the company has a good brand") are not acceptable.

```
INVESTMENT THESIS:
------------------
1. [Competitive advantage or market position]: [Specific data point — e.g., "Market share 38% in steel rebar vs. #2 player at 12%; scale advantage drives COGS/tonne ~$40 below smaller peers."]

2. [Financial metric supporting investment case]: [Specific number + context — e.g., "ROE averaged 22% over past 5 years on D/E of 0.8; earnings quality confirmed: OCF/Net Income = 0.91 (5-year average)."]

3. [Catalyst or near-term trigger]: [Time-bound, specific — e.g., "SBV credit quota for 2025 set at 15%; TCB's CASA ratio (47%) means marginal cost of funds < competitors by ~120bps → NIM expansion 15-25bps in H2 2025 when full rate transmission occurs."]
```

---

### Block 3: Anti-Thesis — 2 Bullets (Required — MUST Complete Before Submitting)

The anti-thesis section is not optional. Every investment has a failure scenario. Seer and Planner require this to assess downside risk. The analyst who skips this has not done the analysis.

```
ANTI-THESIS (FAILURE SCENARIOS):
---------------------------------
1. [Biggest risk to the thesis]: [Specific, data-backed — e.g., "Chinese HRC (hot-rolled coil) export prices are currently 18% below Vietnam domestic prices; sustained dumping would force HPG to cut domestic prices by 10-15% → EBITDA/tonne falls below break-even at current utilization rates."]

2. [Scenario under which thesis fails entirely]: [Structural, not just cyclical — e.g., "If Vietnam's urbanization rate decelerates (current 36%, target 50% by 2030), construction steel demand growth stalls; HPG's Dung Quat Phase 2 expansion CapEx becomes a permanent drag rather than a growth enabler — capital misallocation at scale."]
```

---

### Block 4: Catalyst — 1 Bullet (Required)

The catalyst is the specific event or development that triggers the investment thesis within 6-12 months. It must be time-bounded and observable (when it happens, you will know it happened).

```
CATALYST:
----------
[Specific, time-bounded, observable trigger — e.g., "Q4 2024 earnings release (expected Feb 2025): if pre-sales data shows >20% quarter-on-quarter recovery in signed contracts, the market should re-rate the developer 15-25% as VAS revenue recognition catches up in 2-3 quarters. Catalyst date: February 2025."]
```

|-------------|---------|------------|-----------|--------|------|
| [Year-2]    | [X,XXX] | [XXX]      | [X,XXX]   | [XXX]  | [X.X]|
| [Year-1]    | [X,XXX] | [XXX]      | [X,XXX]   | [XXX]  | [X.X]|

### Block 6: Output Handoff Note (Required)

```
HANDOFF NOTE:
--------------
Thesis quality: [STRONG / MODERATE / WEAK] — [one sentence rationale]
Submit to Seer: [YES / CONDITIONAL / NO]
  Condition (if conditional): [what must improve before Seer should act]

Submit to Planner: YES (always — Planner needs both cases)
Key Planner flag: [Balance sheet risk: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW] — [one sentence on primary risk]

VAS adjustments applied: [YES (list) / NO]
Earnings quality confirmation: OCF/NI = [X.X] — [PASS / FLAG]
Anti-thesis severity: [MANAGEABLE / SIGNIFICANT / THESIS-BREAKING]
```

---

## Quality Gate — Before Submitting

Run this checklist. Do not submit if any item is unchecked.

- [ ] All Block 1 fields complete (no "N/A" or blank fields — investigate if data unavailable)
- [ ] Intrinsic value estimate includes method and assumptions
- [ ] Earnings quality check completed (OCF/Net Income calculated)
- [ ] Thesis has 3 bullets (minimum); each bullet has at least one specific data point
- [ ] Anti-thesis has exactly 2 bullets (not 1, not 0); second bullet addresses total thesis failure
- [ ] Catalyst is time-bounded (not "eventually" or "when conditions improve")
- [ ] 3-year financials table: two actual years, one labeled estimate year
- [ ] VAS adjustments documented where applied
- [ ] Handoff note confirms Seer and Planner routing decision

## Example — HPG Abbreviated

```
COMPANY RESEARCH SUMMARY
========================
Company:   Hoa Phat Group JSC
Ticker:    HPG
Exchange:  HOSE
Sector:    Manufacturing
Sub-sector: Integrated Steel

Market cap:     VND 152,000B  |  USD ~$6.1B  |  Large cap
Current price:  VND 27,800
52-week range:  VND 22,100 — VND 31,500
Date:           2024-11-15

KEY RATIOS:
P/E (trailing):     18.5×  |  Sector avg: 14×
P/B:                2.1×   |  Own historical avg (5yr): 2.3×
ROE (trailing):     11.8%  |  Threshold: >15% = quality
D/E:                0.72   |  Note: conservative
Current ratio:      1.6    |  Note: adequate
FCF yield:          2.9%   |  Note: expensive on FCF basis (CapEx-heavy expansion phase)
OCF/Net Income:     0.74   |  Quality: Moderate (CapEx intensity explains gap)

Intrinsic value estimate:  VND 32,000 — Method: EV/EBITDA peer comparison (8× forward EBITDA)
Margin of safety:          -13% (current price above intrinsic; wait for better entry)
Earnings quality flag:     PASS (CapEx-heavy; OCF gap expected)

INVESTMENT THESIS:
------------------
1. Vietnam's only vertically integrated steel producer with blast furnace capacity; 38% domestic rebar market share vs. #2 at 11%; scale drives cost per tonne VND 500,000 below domestic competitors.

2. Dung Quat Phase 2 (4.5M tonne HRC) expected operational 2025; will make HPG Vietnam's first HRC producer, eliminating the country's largest steel import category (~$3B/year).

3. SBV credit easing → construction activity recovery expected H1 2025 → HPG volume growth 15-20% from current 65% utilization; every 10% volume increase = ~30% EBITDA increase at fixed-cost-heavy operations.

ANTI-THESIS:
------------
1. Chinese HRC export price currently 18% below Vietnam domestic; sustained dumping would force HPG to price match → EBITDA/tonne < $50 → ROE falls below 10% → P/B should compress to 1.2-1.5×.

2. Dung Quat Phase 2 faces greenfield execution risk; any 12+ month delay extends the CapEx drag period; combined with Chinese competition, a Phase 2 delay + dumping scenario implies HPG earnings flat for 3 years → terminal multiple compression.

CATALYST:
---------
Q1 2025 construction activity data (GSO, expected April 2025): credit acceleration visible in mortgage data → HPG Q1 2025 volume guidance revised upward → re-rating.

FINANCIALS (VND Billion):
| Year     | Revenue | Net Income | EPS (VND) | FCF     | D/E  |
|----------|---------|------------|-----------|---------|------|
| 2022     | 142,335 | 8,444      | 1,470     | -3,200  | 0.61 |
| 2023     | 115,823 | 6,875      | 1,196     | 1,100   | 0.68 |
| 2024 E*  | 128,000 | 8,200      | 1,427     | -1,500  | 0.72 |
* Estimate based on H1 2024 actual + H2 consensus (SSI Research model)

HANDOFF NOTE:
Thesis quality: MODERATE — valuation not compelling at current price; monitor for better entry
Submit to Seer: CONDITIONAL — submit when HPG price at VND 24,000-25,000 (30% margin of safety)
Submit to Planner: YES
Key Planner flag: Balance sheet risk: LOW (D/E 0.72, current ratio 1.6, adequate headroom)
VAS adjustments applied: NO (manufacturing; VAS distortions minimal)
Anti-thesis severity: SIGNIFICANT (Chinese dumping risk is real and ongoing)
```
