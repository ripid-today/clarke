## Gold Market

**SJC vs. World Gold:**
- Vietnam government controls SJC (Saigon Jewelry Company) gold bar prices
- SJC typically trades at a significant premium to world gold (has been 5-20M VND/tael premium)
- Premium reflects domestic hoarding demand + import restrictions + government pricing
- PNJ (Phu Nhuan Jewelry) gold tracks world gold more closely than SJC

**Planner's view on gold:** Gold is a legitimate inflation hedge and safe-haven allocation for Vietnam retail investors. A 5-10% allocation in SJC bars (for domestic safety) or PNJ jewelry gold is reasonable for conservative portfolios. Do not use SJC premium as investment thesis — it's a policy artifact, not a market signal.

## Social Insurance (BHXH)

**Contribution rates (employee + employer, as of 2026):**
- Employee: 8% of salary (to pension + sickness + maternity)
- Employer: 17.5% of salary (to same pool + work accident)
- Total: 25.5% of salary to BHXH

**Pension eligibility:** 20+ years of contribution + age 60 (men), 55 (women) — transitioning to 62/60 under labor law reform

**Early withdrawal:** Possible after leaving employment (no new BHXH contribution for 12+ months). Lump sum = number of years × 1.5× average monthly salary. **Planner's advice:** Avoid early withdrawal — it forfeits future pension rights permanently.

## Tax Rules for Investors

| Tax | Rate | Applies To |
|-----|------|-----------|
| Securities transfer tax | 0.1% per sell trade | All stock sales, regardless of gain/loss |
| Dividend withholding | 5% | Dividends from listed companies |
| PIT on stock gains | 0% (currently) | Capital gains from stock sales exempt (only 0.1% transfer tax applies) |
| PIT brackets (employment) | 5-35% progressive | Only on salary/wages, not investment income |

**Key implication:** Vietnam's 0.1% per-sell-trade tax (not per-buy) means frequent trading erodes returns. A trader who turns over portfolio 12× per year pays 1.2% in transfer taxes alone — above the risk-free rate. **Planner always flags this in position sizing: holding period matters.**

## Mortgage and Credit Context

**Typical mortgage rates (HCMC property, 2026):**
- State bank rates: 7-9% per annum (promotional 12-24 month rate 5-6%, then reverts)
- Commercial bank rates: 9-12% standard
- Important: Vietnamese mortgages often have variable rates after the fixed period

**Consumer loan rates:** 15-25% per annum (personal unsecured loans at consumer finance companies like FE Credit, HD Saison)

**CIC Credit Scoring:** Vietnam Credit Information Center scores range 1-5 (1=best, 5=bad). Missing any payment for 10+ days creates a negative CIC record lasting 5 years. Planner always asks: does Clarke have any CIC concerns that would affect borrowing capacity?
