# Bear Case Construction — Planner Reference

## Purpose
How to construct the counter-thesis to Seer's bull case. A bear case without structure is pessimism; with structure it is risk-adjusted evidence.

## Bear Case Structure (4 Required Fields)

Every Planner submission to Commander must include all 4 fields:

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Risk Score** | X/10 with component breakdown |
| **Top 3 Risks** | Specific, evidenced (not generic "market risk") |
| **Downside Scenario** | Bear case price target with method (P/E compression + EPS haircut) |
| **Maximum Safe Position** | % of portfolio (0% if risk score ≥9) |

## Devil's Advocate Checklist

Before finalizing the bear case, work through all 5 questions:

| Question | What to Look For |
|----------|-----------------|
| **1. Valuation — Is this already priced in?** | If the thesis catalyst is well-known (analyst coverage, news coverage), the stock may already reflect it. Check: is current P/E already pricing in the growth? |
| **2. Execution — Can management deliver?** | Track record: has management met guidance in the last 3 years? If miss rate >30%, the thesis depends on management improvement — a secondary risk. |
| **3. Macro — What if SBV tightens?** | Run sensitivity: if refinancing rate +1%, what happens to borrowing cost, net interest margin (banks), and consumer demand? |
| **4. Regulatory — Sector policy reversal risk?** | What government actions in the last 3 years have affected this sector? Is the current policy durable? |
| **5. Accounting — Does the cash flow confirm the earnings?** | OCF/Net income ratio < 0.7 = earnings quality concern. Is revenue recognition aggressive? Are receivables growing faster than revenue? |

**Rule:** Every "I don't know" in this checklist adds +1 to the risk score. Unknown risks are real risks.

## Risk Score Component Breakdown

The risk score (1-10) is constructed from 3 components:

| Component | Max Contribution | What It Measures |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Downside valuation** | 0-4 points | Severity of the bear case price target (% below current price) |
| **Red flags** | 0-3 points | Number and severity of balance sheet/accounting red flags |
| **Macro risk** | 0-3 points | Macro environment alignment with bear case |

**Downside valuation scoring:**
- Bear case price >20% below current: +1
- Bear case price 20-35% below current: +2
- Bear case price 35-50% below current: +3
- Bear case price >50% below current: +4

**Red flag scoring:**
- 1-2 minor red flags: +1
- 3+ minor OR 1 major (e.g., auditor qualified opinion, offshore RPT): +2
- Multiple major red flags: +3

**Macro risk scoring:**
- Neutral macro: +0
- Adverse macro for this sector: +1
- Macro headwinds (SBV tightening + VND weakness + EM outflows): +2
- Severe macro risk (currency crisis, credit crunch): +3

## Position Size Recommendations

| Risk Score | Maximum Position Size | Notes |
|------------|----------------------|-------|
| 1-3 | Seer's suggested max | Planner defers |
| 4-5 | Seer's max (flag concerns) | Include specific risk notes in submission |
| 6 | Min(Seer's max, 7%) | Cap at 7% |
| 7 | Min(Seer's max × 0.5, 5%) | Half Seer's recommendation |
| 8 | Min(Seer's max × 0.25, 3%) | Quarter Seer's recommendation |
| 9-10 | 0% — Hard stop | Veto. Commander can override only with written rationale from Clarke. |

## Common Construction Errors (Avoid These)

- **Vague risks:** "Market could go down" is not a risk — "SBV tightening +100bp would compress this bank's NIM by 40bp, reducing EPS by 15%" is a risk
- **Missing bear case price:** Always state the bear case price with method — the number grounds the discussion
- **Generic downside:** "Company could miss earnings" — specify the mechanism: which line item, what scenario
- **Underestimating Vietnam-specific risk:** Related-party loans, pledged shares, and VAS revenue recognition issues are systematically underreported. Default to +1 on red flag score if you cannot confirm these are absent.
